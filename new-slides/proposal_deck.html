Exact-duplicate check found 48. Near-duplicate check found 4,282.
One stripped subject line hid 99% of the contamination. Normal deduplication misses it.
That “good transfer” was memorisation, not generalisation.

Gap 4 · size ≠ quality
0.58

It flags almost every email as phishing. 3× the price, worse results.
The small 3B model held 0.80 to 0.84 on all three corpora. It never collapsed.
15 points worse in-corpus, far more robust across corpora, for half a cent.
Nobody reports accuracy, robustness and cost in the same table, but that is exactly what a deployment decision needs.
